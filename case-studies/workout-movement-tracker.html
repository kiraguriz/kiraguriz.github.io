01 / Problem

Fitness workflows get split apart.

Fitness apps often split movement analysis, nutrition, goals, and coaching into disconnected workflows. This project explored a tighter mobile experience where local health data and AI suggestions support daily training decisions.

The real constraint is persistence: meals, workouts, body metrics, goals, and AI outputs have to survive beyond a single chat-like interaction.

02 / Approach

Make the coach data-aware.

① Local data first

SQLite repositories own meals, workouts, body metrics, goals, and saved AI results.

② AI in the workflow

Gemini supports daily tips, workout ideas, diet advice, feedback, and weekly goal adjustments.

③ Health context

Goal calculations, food lookup, and activity sync paths connect recommendations to tracked inputs.

03 / System

A mobile app with a memory.

The app keeps structured local records, enriches food and health context, and passes bounded data into AI coaching flows.
