Case study

Workout Movement Tracker

A cross-platform fitness app that combines workout tracking, diet support, AI coaching, local persistence, and device health integrations.

RoleLead developer in a four-person team
StackFlutter, Dart, SQLite, Gemini, ML Kit
FocusAI coaching, data model, diet flow, goals, UI
PlatformiOS and Android
DataMeals, workouts, body metrics, goals
AICoaching and adaptive suggestions

Problem

Fitness apps often split movement analysis, nutrition, goals, and coaching into disconnected workflows. This project explored a tighter mobile experience where local health data and AI suggestions can support daily training decisions.

Technical work

  • Integrated Gemini AI for daily tips, workout suggestions, diet advice, post-session feedback, and weekly goal adjustments.
  • Built a SQLite data layer with migrations and repositories for meals, workouts, body metrics, goals, and AI result persistence.
  • Implemented USDA FoodData Central lookup with debounced search, enrichment, and offline-first local caching.
  • Added evidence-based goal calculations using BMR formulas and activity sync paths.

What it shows

This is the clearest evidence that I can own cross-platform app work beyond UI: data modeling, API integration, AI features, local caching, and team-level implementation discipline.
