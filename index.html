01 / About

How I got here.

I came to software the way most of my classmates did — through curiosity about the apps on my phone. What kept me here was harder to name: somewhere between the satisfaction of a clean database migration and the strange comfort of a green test suite at 1 a.m.

The work I'm proudest of tends to be quiet. Apps that don't lose your data. APIs that don't surprise their callers. Coaching features that admit when the model is unsure. I'd rather ship the smallest reliable thing and earn the next piece than build a demo that does ten things badly.

Lately I've been thinking a lot about how AI fits into products without becoming the product. I want models to feel like good colleagues — show their work, ask before doing anything destructive, fail gracefully.
